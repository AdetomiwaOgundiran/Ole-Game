# Olè - The Lagos Hustle | Release Notes

## Version 2.0.0 - December 2024

### What's New

#### Clearer Obstacles
- **Wire obstacles are now unmistakable** - Added visible barbed wire on top with red spikes and a caution bar, plus a full wire mesh filling the middle. Players can instantly see that sliding is the only way through.
- **Traffic Spikes stand out** - Renamed from "Thorns" and redesigned in bright yellow so they're easy to spot against the road. No more blending in!
- **Updated tutorial images** - The instruction manual now shows the new obstacle designs so players know what to expect.

#### Smoother Gameplay
- **Consistent speed progression** - Fixed an issue where the game felt like it slowed down around 1100 points. Speed now increases steadily throughout the entire run.

#### Better Mobile Experience
- **Smaller, repositioned controls** - Touch controls are now more compact and positioned lower on screen, giving you a clearer view of incoming obstacles.
- **Streamlined score display** - Score boxes are smaller and less intrusive on mobile screens.

#### Weekly Challenges
- **Fresh leaderboard** - Week 2 challenge is now live! Previous week's scores have been archived. Compete for the top spot on the new leaderboard.

## Version 1.0.0 - December 2024

### Launch Features
- Endless runner gameplay set in the streets of Lagos
- Collect money, phones, and food while dodging obstacles
- Three-lane movement with jump and slide actions
- Global leaderboard to compete with other players
- Mobile-friendly touch controls
- Immersive audio with background music and sound effects
